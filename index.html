What the data showed

A record year, then a loss quarter

Rs 17,175 crore of profit in 2025-26, the highest ever, and a loss of Rs 11,526 crore eleven weeks later. Refining was strong in that quarter, so the swing came from marketing margin.

Gross refining margin 23.80 dollars a barrel, against 3.08 a year earlier

The network is outrunning the fuel

16,476 outlets became 25,131. Volume grew about 7 percent over a comparable span. Opening more outlets raises reach and lowers the average at the same time.

52 percent against 7 percent, March 2020 to May 2026

Ten states hold most of the network

A national average describes almost no real state, so it cannot be used as a territory target without being too soft in one place and too hard in another.

71.4 percent in ten states. Uttar Pradesh 13,317, Lakshadweep 4

Price is not a lever sales holds

The pump price is set by policy. What a sales officer can work is the mix: which products, which customers, which sites.

About Rs 2,400 crore a day absorbed by the industry, March 2026

How it was built

Read each source document itself, never a summary of it.
Log every figure with its value, its as on date and whether it was verified.
Reconcile the conflicts. The outlet count appears five different ways, and all five are correct at different dates.
Model it. 21 tables and about 2,200 rows in a star schema, 10 relationships, all single direction.
Write 89 DAX measures, grouped by the page each one serves.
Build every page the same way: a headline, six KPI cards, what it means, the charts, and a footer naming the sources.

One thing to be clear about

Two of the eight pages use illustrative data. HPCL publishes no outlet level figures to anyone outside the company, so those outlet, distributor and customer numbers are invented for demonstration, and both pages say so on screen. Their structure is real. Every other page uses published figures only.
